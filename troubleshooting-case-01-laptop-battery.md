# Scenario: A user reports that their laptop will not turn on, or it shuts down shortly after being unplugged.

Your task: Investigate whether the battery could be contributing to the problem and document the troubleshooting steps you would take.

**ANSWER:** 

**1. Problem Description**

A user reports that their laptop shuts down when disconnected from its charger.

**2. Possible Causes**

- A depleted or degraded battery
- A faulty charger or charging connection
- A battery connection issue
- Another hardware or power-management problem

**3. Troubleshooting Steps**

1. Ask the user what happens when the charger is connected.
2. Check whether the charger and charging cable are connected securely.
3. Observe whether the laptop recognizes the battery and reports its charge level.
4. Check the manufacturer's battery health diagnostics, if available.
5. Consult the laptop manufacturer's service manual before inspecting or replacing the battery.

**4. Conclusion**

- Follow the manufacturer's instructions before removing a battery.
- Do not handle or reinstall a swollen, leaking, or damaged battery.
- Do not open a laptop or disconnect internal components while it is powered.
- Use a compatible replacement battery approved for the laptop model.

**5. Findings**

No physical testing has been performed. This is a simulated troubleshooting scenario.

**6. Conclusion**

Further diagnostic checks are needed before confirming that the battery is faulty. If testing confirms battery failure, replacement may be appropriate.
